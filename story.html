i. The struggle

Shopping was the unexpected challenge.

As a Muslim girl growing up in the West, alongside all the other challenges young diaspora face, came one I never saw coming: shopping.

Finding clothes with enough coverage was no easy feat. Walking into a shop and finding a stylish full-length top, or a dress that ticks every box? Nearly impossible.

ii. The frustration

The same designs. The same nudes.

I can't deny there are other Australian modest clothing lines out there. But I'm sure we've all seen enough of the identical nude palettes across the board.

We're sick of seeing the same designs over and over again.

iii. Taking matters into my own hands

So I made what I wanted.

Years later, I stopped waiting for someone else to design it. I took matters into my own hands and created the clothes I wanted to wear myself.

I'm sure this isn't an issue I alone face, and it's something I feel obligated to share with you all.

iv. The Dalaa philosophy

Modesty, on your own terms.

دلع (Dalaa) is the feeling of being adored, pampered, a little spoiled and a little extra, in the best way. That's how I want you to feel in what you wear.

Every piece is designed with intention, individuality and an appreciation for the details. Because modesty should never mean compromising on personal style.

Because modesty was never meant to mean compromising on who you are.
